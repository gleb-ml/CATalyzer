Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2025

CATalyze is built with a privacy-first mindset. We believe your data belongs to you — not to us. This extension was designed from the ground up so that we never see, touch, or store anything about you or your usage.

Data We Don't Collect

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Account data or identifiers
  • Analytics or telemetry
  • Cookies or tracking pixels
  • User behavior, clicks, or interaction patterns
  • The text you highlight or send to AI providers

How CATalyze Works

CATalyze runs entirely inside your browser. There are no CATalyze servers — the extension has no backend, no database, and no cloud infrastructure of its own.

Your API key is stored exclusively in chrome.storage.local on your device. It never leaves your machine except when sent directly to your chosen AI provider to authenticate a request.

When you highlight text and activate the extension, that text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected (Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a custom URL) via their official API. CATalyze acts only as a bridge — it does not intercept, log, or store this communication.

How that provider handles your request is governed by their own privacy policy, not ours.

Third-Party Services

Depending on which provider you configure, your text may be processed by one of the following services. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Groq groq.com/privacy-policy
  • OpenAI openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • OpenRouter openrouter.ai/privacy

Permissions

CATalyze requests the following browser permissions, each with a specific purpose:

  • contextMenus — adds the «Explain with AI» option to the right-click menu when you select text
  • sidePanel — displays the CATalyze interface in Chrome's built-in side panel
  • storage — saves your settings and API key locally on your device via chrome.storage.local
  • activeTab — reads the text you have selected on the current page when you invoke the extension

Your Data, Your Control

You are in full control of everything CATalyze stores. You can remove your API key at any time through the extension settings. Uninstalling CATalyze from Chrome permanently deletes all locally stored data — settings, API key, and preferences — with no residual trace.
